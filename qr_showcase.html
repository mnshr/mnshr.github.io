💡 Presentation Best Practices for QR Codes
Use SVG in Keynote / PowerPoint: Drag and drop the .svg file directly into your slide editor. It stays razor sharp at any projection scale (4K/8K).
Screen Projection Size: Ensure the QR code occupies at least 200–250 pixels on screen so attendees in the back row can scan it effortlessly.
Error Correction Level H: Built with 30% Reed-Solomon recovery capacity, guaranteeing rapid scan recognition even with the center LinkedIn badge.
Keep Margin Intact: The quiet zone (white/dark padding) around the code is crucial for optical sensor edge-detection. Do not crop the borders.
Mobile App Deep-Linking: Encoded with https://www.linkedin.com/in/mhira to trigger iOS Universal Links & Android App Links. If your phone opens the web browser instead of the app, simply tap "Open in app" at the top banner in Safari/Chrome once to set the native app as your default deep-link handler.
